# eFlow Mobile: client walkthrough and page guide

**Source reviewed: 17 November 2026.** This guide describes the current mobile checkout, including changes that may not yet be in the APK you have installed. It is a guide to the screens and rules, not a claim of signed-in acceptance on Android or iOS. Features that depend on a shared service still require that service to be available for the account and record.

## The 30-second explanation

“Home tells you what to look at first. My Work shows what is assigned to you. Reviews shows decisions you can make, and Inbox shows updates you received. Projects gives you the full picture for work you can access. The buttons change based on your appointment, the record’s current status, and what the system has verified. For example, a contributor can submit work, a Lead can review a contributor’s submission, and an independent reviewer may still need to approve final Task completion.”

| Account | Bottom tabs in the current checkout | What changes with the role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head | Home · Reviews · My Work · Projects · More | Home highlights Office decisions and project risk. Office Analytics and Office Budget require the relevant Office access. |
| Member | Home · Reviews · My Work · Projects · More | Home highlights personal assignments. A Member appointed as a Lead or reviewer may receive actions for that specific work. |
| Accounting Staff | Home · Reviews · My Work · Petty cash · More | Home highlights financial actions. Projects and Office budget & releases are available through More when authorized. |
| Administrator | No mobile sign-in | System administration is handled on eFlow Web. |

Use the **bell on Home** to open Inbox. A notification can open the exact Task, Subtask, conversation or financial record if the link still resolves and access can be verified. Mobile starts in the account’s own Office context rather than offering a general workspace picker. Opening an authorized record can take you into its context. Back normally takes you through the stack where you opened it; opening a link does not grant new access.

## Sign-in, invitations and account activation

| Screen | What the client sees | Why the form accepts or refuses an action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Startup and sign-in | eFlow startup screen, then email, password, show/hide password, remember-session option and Sign in. A slow request can show progress guidance. | The email is required and must be in a valid format; a password is required. A connection is needed to sign in. The server confirms credentials, account status and mobile role. Sign-in is protected against duplicate taps. |
| **You’re invited** | Opened from an original invitation. Shows the invited email, Office or project access and the relevant account steps. Existing users sign in or accept with the matching session; new users may provide a name and password. | The link must still be valid and match the account. When required, full name is 2–160 characters; new passwords are 12–128 characters and must match confirmation. Some project guest invitations lead to a profile/access step. |
| **Activate your account** | Name, email, Office and role associated with the activation link, followed by new password and confirmation. | The service validates the link. Password must be 12–128 characters and match confirmation. A used, expired or invalid link needs the secure account process. |
| **Your project access is ready** | After eligible guest onboarding, the user can add optional skills, work experience, a professional summary or a private Personal Data Sheet (PDS), then select **Continue to project**. | A PDS must be a PDF up to 10 MB. The account must remain signed in, and the server must confirm completion of the invitation step. Only authorized viewers can open the PDS. |
| **Project invitation** (shared project link) | The signed-in recipient can try to open the shared project. | The link and session are checked for that project; the app cannot open unrelated or unauthorized work. |
| Access unavailable / link unavailable / web handoff | An explanation, and a retry, back or **Open in eFlow Web** action when appropriate. | Common reasons are an unsupported link, an incomplete link, changed access, or a function handled on Web. The correct record and authorization still apply on Web. |

## Home: why an item is at the top

| Home view | Contents and order | Why an action appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Head** | Summary counts for current projects, the decisions preview and Office work at risk. The decisions preview shows up to **three** entries: urgent eligible items such as assigned overdue work or cash needed by today come ahead of routine reviews, followed by other eligible actions such as assigning a Lead. Within a priority band the app compares its action date, then uses a stable tie-break. The **Current Projects** preview also shows up to **three**: projects with overdue/blocked work first, then on-hold projects within the same risk group, then by title. Completed, archived and cancelled projects are outside this preview. | A card links to the relevant work or review. An Office Analytics shortcut requires Office Head appointment and reporting access. Office Budget opens the authorized Office budget view. A project appearing first is a risk signal, not an approval. |
| **Member** | **Needs your action** shows up to **three** open assignments in this order: **changes requested → overdue → due today → blocker reported → other open work**. Within the same tier, earlier due dates come first, with undated work last. **Coming up** shows up to three other assignments due from today through seven days ahead, earliest first; it excludes the priority rows and work awaiting review. **Latest updates** shows the three newest updates. **Waiting on others** shows up to three assignments awaiting review or blocked without an action available to you, ordered by due date. | **Continue work** or **Review requested changes** is shown when the user can act as the appointed Lead. Otherwise the card can say **Open assigned task/subtask**. Being on this list does not by itself allow a direct change. |
| **Accounting Staff** | **Action required** previews up to three authorized financial actions: scheduled releases first, earlier schedule first; receipt/settlement review actions follow, ordered by their submission date within the same priority. The **Outstanding transactions** preview shows up to five remaining open transactions ordered by needed-by date, with missing dates last; it excludes requests already represented by the release or settlement actions. **Cash & budget position** shows available budget, release capacity and amounts such as released and pending release when official figures are available. | Release and settlement cards depend on separate permissions. The queue shortcut requires release queue access. An unavailable balance or missing fiscal budget is shown as such. |

**Client example:** A Member’s Task returned for changes can appear above another Task that is already overdue. In My Work, the same two records can appear in a different order because that page primarily groups assignments by date.

## My Work, Reviews and Inbox

| Page | What is shown and how it is ordered | Buttons and validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My Work** | Eligible assignments, including tasks and subtasks, with title, project/Office or branch context, assignment role, status and due information when available. Order: **Overdue → Today → Upcoming → Unscheduled → Completed → Other history**. Open groups sort by due date, then title; Completed and Other history sort by last update, newest first. Date groups use the work’s calendar. Search can match title, project/workspace, Office, parent Task, branch path or assignment role. | The filter selects a group; search and the filter are applied before paging. Tapping a row opens that exact Task/Subtask. This is an assignment list, so a readable project record may appear in Projects without appearing here. A pending-review assignment can remain visible even though execution is unavailable. |
| **Reviews** | A queue of assigned decisions: eligible final Task reviews, Subtask submissions, assigned personal work reviews and eligible financial reviews or Head escalations. The filter narrows to **All, Tasks, Subtasks, Personal or Financial**. The queue combines decision sources; it does not apply a single newest-first order across all types. Assigned Office Subtask proposals arrive oldest submission first within that source. | Opening a row goes to its decision panel. Approve or Request changes appears only for a current eligible reviewer. Self-review and superseded decisions are restricted. A cash-related review may show that it is waiting for settlement. |
| **Inbox** | Updates are **newest first**, regardless of read status in All. Use **All / Unread / Read**, categories **Work / Messages / Finance / Collaboration**, or search. Some update types appear only under All types. The unread indicator counts updates, not outstanding decisions. | **Mark all as read** requires unread updates and confirmation; it covers unread updates outside the current filter as well. Opening or marking an update read does not approve the underlying work. A removed or unauthorized record cannot be opened through an old notice. |

Default **Current projects** excludes archived records; use **All statuses** or a specific status to find them. Planning records are limited to the eligible Head’s own Office context. The pinned **Project** column stays visible when swiping across **Status, Progress, Lead office, Project lead, Schedule and Target date**. | Search matches project title. The star changes your favorites; it does not change the project or anyone’s access. Select the project name to open it. Unavailable facts are shown separately from zero progress. |

Tasks follow the project’s group order and saved row position; ungrouped tasks appear in their own section. This is why the top row need not be the most urgent task. | Search narrows Tasks in the current view. **Add task** appears on the active project’s Main table when the Head has verified creation authority and a task group is available. Task title, date range and prerequisite rules are checked on save. |
| **Board / Agenda** | Board groups the same Tasks by workflow status: pending assignment, to do, in progress, for review, changes requested, completed, cancelled. Audit Trail lists recorded project/Task changes with actors, times and event details. Newest events appear first. Use search and the activity category filter to narrow the list, or page through older history. | Use Audit Trail to see what was recorded, including changes from other authorized users. A missing or filtered event is not confirmation that an action succeeded. |
| **Project options: People, Participating Offices, Conversations and Files** | People shows project appointments and membership; Participating Offices shows Office involvement. Conversations opens the project’s authorized chat. Files shows authorized attached files, with search and **All / Documents / Images / Ready** filters. | Opening a project does not let a user staff it, change Office participation or manage files. | **Project options: Funding / Complete / Archive** | Funding shows authorized project allocations and closeout information. | **Personal project / task** | Opened from an authorized assignment, link or available project listing. A title and eligible Lead are required; a reviewer must be a different eligible person. The Lead can start and record progress (a whole number from 0 to 99) in the relevant state. An appointed independent reviewer sees **Review work** after submission and must add a note to return it. A Lead without a required reviewer can complete directly where allowed. Personal permissions do not imply Office finance authority. |

## Task and Subtask: what each button means

**Task details** is the work record: title and status, project, dates, priority, completion, Lead/contributors, prerequisites, description and definition of done where recorded, Subtasks, and entry points to Activity, Evidence & review, Funding, Files and Task conversation. **Subtask details** adds its parent and branch context, its own dates/priority, Lead/contributors, prerequisite and child work, activity and submission controls. The inline Subtask preview shows up to four; longer lists offer **See all**. The branch follows the saved structure. This checkout reserves the pre-start Office Subtask planning view for the parent Task Lead; after parent start, authorized viewers can open the branch. Full enforcement across Web, links and feeds depends on the shared backend policies.

| Control or state | When it appears and what it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start work / Resume work** | The appointed Task Lead sees Start for eligible To do work after the planned start is reached and prerequisites are satisfied. Resume is for work returned for changes. An eligible Subtask Lead must also have a started parent/ancestor as applicable. Start asks for confirmation; the service can still refuse if the authoritative date, status, prerequisite or permission rules fail. |
| **Update progress / Update work** | Available after work starts for eligible participants. An authorized Lead can record allowed progress or a blocker; a contributor submits a proposal for the eligible Lead. The form may include work state, work note, blocker, next step and evidence. A blocked state needs an explanation. A pending proposal does not change official progress. |
| **Mark as complete / Submit for review** | A Lead can initiate completion once the prerequisites and applicable child checks are clear. Final Office Task completion still goes through independent review. A contributor submits for review; a separate Subtask Lead may also need a parent Lead decision. The completion form captures a note when required and supports evidence. A pending review is not a completed item. |
| **Approve / Request changes** | A current eligible reviewer can inspect submitted work, evidence, submitter and submission history. A request for changes requires feedback. The submitter cannot review their own work. For a contributor’s Subtask proposal the service uses the nearest eligible Lead, with authorized fallback when necessary. Final Office Task review follows the responsible Office’s independent review rule. Unsettled cash may block a completion approval. |
| **Edit / Add subtask / Delete** | These appear only for the relevant record and appointment. Task names are required (up to 300 characters); dates must be valid and start cannot follow due. Task dates must fit the project schedule; Subtask dates must fit parent/ancestor bounds. The app prevents self-links/cycles and checks eligible prerequisites. After start, only permitted corrections are offered; supervising authority and a reason are needed for certain due date changes. Structure, planned start and prerequisites are locked after started history. Delete is restricted to eligible unstarted work, asks for confirmation and can be refused if the service cannot verify it. |
| **Manage team / Save team** | Lead appointment and contributor management are separate permissions. An appointed Lead can manage their own contributors where authorized; changing the Lead requires separate authority. Pickers show eligible people; confirming a picker stages the choice, and Save team is the action that writes it. |
| **Activity / Evidence & review / Files** | Activity shows recorded updates, actions, comments and changes, newest first in the Task/Subtask timelines. Evidence & review shows submitted versions and decisions. Files open only if the attachment remains accessible. Project or work context may be read-only even when the user can see the history. |
| **Funding** | Opens the funding and cash history for the exact Task or Subtask. The Project Lead allocates authorized project funds to Tasks; the Task Lead distributes approved Task funds to Subtasks where permitted. A funding action requires the relevant permission, available balance and valid record state. |

**How completion is calculated:** A project shows completed Tasks out of the counted project Tasks; a Task or Subtask with children shows how many direct children are complete. For example, **2 of 4 = 50%**. A leaf with no children shows 100% when officially complete, otherwise 0% in the completion display. A progress note, a submission awaiting review and money spent do not count as completed work. The details can still show a separate recorded work update or financial value.

**If a prerequisite edit is refused:** The mobile picker can offer eligible prerequisites with overlapping dates or no due date, but the installed service may still reject those combinations. The app reports the failed save rather than applying the change locally. Selecting a prerequisite never marks that prerequisite complete.

## Office Budget, requests, receipts and settlement

| Page or panel | What the client sees | Why buttons appear or are withheld |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Office Budget / Office budget & releases** | Office and fiscal year scope, cash workflow counts and budget figures. The summary distinguishes allocated budget, available balance, commitments, released cash, pending releases and settled expenditure. Additional sections may show annual release history and daily/receipt controls. Fiscal years are listed newest first when selectable. | Head access to a budget summary is separate from Accounting execution. A context control appears only when there is an authorized choice. Journal authoring and financial configuration are handled on Web. |
| **Requests & releases** | Accounting queue, newest-created requests first within the authorized Office/year. Search by reference or purpose; use stages such as **To release, Receipts, Review and Settled**. A row shows purpose, reference, status and the relevant amount for that stage: requested, authorized, released or settled. | This queue needs authorized Accounting access. Tap a row for the exact request, its releases/vouchers, receipt versions and decisions. The queue is separate from the small prioritized preview on Accounting Home. |
| **Task/Subtask cash request** | Form captures purchase purpose, approved funding sources and amounts, needed-by date and supporting information as applicable. The record distinguishes requested, authorized, released, spent and returned cash. Release entries and receipt versions have their own history. | Funding sources must be valid and selected once; amounts must be positive and within available funding. Needed-by is checked against the work schedule. The requester can correct or cancel only in eligible stages. Lead/Head authorization and Accounting recording of a scheduled release are separate. A cheque release requires its cheque reference. |
| **Receipts and cash return** | After release, the eligible requester can submit receipt files, dates, vendors, item details/expense descriptions, a liquidation note and details of unused cash returned where required. Receipt totals determine declared spending; released cash less declared spending is the unused balance, where known. | The Task cash package requires 1–100 receipt entries and attachments. Dates must be valid, amounts and item totals must reconcile, and spending cannot exceed released cash through this form. Limits and any allowed override reasons depend on the applicable policy. A receipt draft is not a posted expense. |
| **Assigned Financial Reviews / Receipt review and settlement** | The independent reviewer sees the assigned package, request/Office/year, evidence, receipts and spending. Accounting sees settlement controls when eligible. A staffing escalation can open a separate Head decision. | Review, approval and journal posting are separate checks. An authorized Accounting Staff member needs current settlement and journal authority and any required independent approval before posting. Roles, versions, conflicting involvement and receipt/cash-return checks may prevent an action. An unverified result must be checked before trying a new transaction. |
| **Office petty cash** | A **separate Office revolving fund**. Shows a ₱30,000 starting fund, available cash, reserves and outstanding amounts. Eligible Accounting Staff also see replenishment capacity and can replenish when there is room. **All / Action needed / Mine** narrow the authorized list, which follows the fund’s returned order (newest requests first in the shared contract). Head and Accounting can see eligible Office requests; other requesters see their own. | A request/replenishment needs a purpose/reference, valid transaction date and amount within the available balance. The appointed Head can approve/reject or endorse/return receipts when eligible and cannot decide their own request. Accounting can record an approved release or settle an eligible endorsed request. The requester submits receipts after release. Receipt checks include vendor, number, date, file, a **₱5,000 per-receipt limit** and total within the request. Tap a receipt to open its protected file. If the preview browser blocks the new tab, allow pop-ups and retry. |

**Client example:** If ₱5,000 was released and ₱3,500 was recorded as settled expenditure, the release and the expense are different figures. Where the transaction reconciles accordingly, ₱1,500 is unused cash returned. An absent or unverifiable amount is shown as unavailable, not as an official zero. Some finance workflows depend on shared backend reconciliation; an available screen alone does not establish that a live financial write will succeed.

## What to say when a screen looks different

- **“Why does my colleague see a button I don’t?”** Compare the account’s role, Office, appointment on the record, workflow status and whether current access could be checked. For example, assigning contributors and appointing a Lead are different permissions.
- **“Why isn’t this record on my first page?”** Check which list you’re using. Home is a short priority preview; My Work is assignments grouped by date; Projects puts favorites first; Inbox is chronological. Also check filters, archived status and whether you can read the record.
- **“The action is disabled or says it’s checking.”** The app may be verifying permissions, dates, dependencies, balance, receipts or the result of a previous request. Refresh or check the saved request if prompted. A loading screen, empty filter result and unavailable record mean different things.
- **“Is this completed?”** Look for the recorded status and decision. A submission, endorsement, scheduled release or pending receipt is an intermediate step. A success message is only meaningful for the action it names.
- **“Why does this open Web?”** Advanced project setup/planning, detailed reports, identity/access administration and financial configuration remain on eFlow Web. The user’s existing permissions still apply there. Admin sign-in, AI Live, voice and video calls are outside this mobile release.

**Suggested demo:** Use a Member to show Home, then open the same assignment in My Work and explain the two orderings. Open the Task and identify its Lead, prerequisite, status and next action. Switch to a separately authorized reviewer and show how Reviews differs from Inbox. Finish on Projects and swipe the pinned table. For an Accounting audience, add a read-only tour of the budget, request history and separate petty cash fund.
